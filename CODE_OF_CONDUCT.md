# Code of conduct

WinLogs sets up logging for malware analysis, DFIR and detection-engineering labs. Keep issues, pull requests and discussions about that, and treat the people in them with
respect.

## Expected

- Be patient with people who are new to the subject. A question that looks basic is often the one
  someone else was afraid to ask.
- Criticize code, text and decisions, not people.
- Assume good intent, and ask when something is unclear.

## Not acceptable

- Harassment, insults, or personal attacks of any kind.
- Posting anyone's personal information, including your own: names, addresses, account details,
  hostnames, usernames or logs from a real machine.
- Spam, advertising, or sustained off-topic posting.

## What happens

The maintainer may edit or delete comments, close issues and pull requests, or block an account
that does not follow this code. Personal information posted by mistake will be removed as soon as
it is noticed, and you can ask for that yourself.

To report a problem privately, use the repository's **Security** tab and choose **Report a
vulnerability**. It reaches only the maintainer, and it works for conduct reports as well as
security ones.
